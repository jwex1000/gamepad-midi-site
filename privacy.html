Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Gamepad MIDI does not collect any data.

  • It has no accounts.
  • It has no analytics, ads or tracking.
  • It never connects to the internet.
  • It reads your game controller only to send MIDI on your device.
  • Your mapping is saved in your host app's session, on your device.

Nothing leaves your device, so nothing is shared with anyone.
